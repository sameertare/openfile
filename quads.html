👥 Quads Pairings

Import an NWChess RosterTable.csv export, and this splits the field into round-robin groups of 4 (quads) by rating — then runs pairings, results, standings and a wall chart for every quad in parallel.

1 Roster

An NWChess RosterTable.csv export. FIDE ratings ignored; seeded by max(NWSRS, USCF); withdrawn players excluded. Every quad plays a fixed round-robin schedule — no bye requests or family-group avoidance (there's no dynamic pairing to steer).

Player status

Mark a player withdrawn if they've dropped out of the event — they're skipped when pairing every future round. This doesn't change any round already paired; enter their current board as a normal result (or use "Fix a mistake in this round" for it).

3 Standings

Tiebreaks: Buchholz (sum of opponents' scores) then Sonneborn-Berger. Byes score a full point.

🗺 Wall Chart

Each cell is the opponent's current standings number, color, and result — w/b for White/Black, +/−/= for win/loss/draw. Opponent numbers update as standings change.
